Paper to Goodnotes

Take a photo of handwritten notes and get a Goodnotes file with real, editable ink.

Shoot from above with the whole page in view, on a darker surface if you can. Avoid strong shadows.

Your photo and the strokes found in it will show here.
Higher picks up fainter pencil; lower ignores smudges and printed text.
"Photo" copies the thickness of your pen on paper.
Turn off if the photo is already just the page.

Opens in Goodnotes as a whiteboard. In the share sheet, pick Goodnotes. Or save the file and open it from the Files app.
